## Problem Statement

Students often have to manage multiple aspects of their daily life, such as expenses, attendance, academic tasks, and personal schedules. Managing these activities separately can make it difficult to keep track of important information and maintain an organized routine.

The **Smart Student Life Manager** is developed as a Python-based application that provides a simple, menu-driven platform for managing student expenses, monitoring attendance, organizing tasks, and viewing basic analytics.

The purpose of this project is to provide students with a simple and user-friendly system that helps them organize their daily activities and monitor their academic and financial information efficiently.
